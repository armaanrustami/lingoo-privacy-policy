Lingoo Play Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Lingoo Play is a language-learning game. This policy explains what information the app and its third-party services may process.

Information stored in the app

Lingoo Play does not require an account or login. Game progress, preferences, and other information created during play are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this locally stored information through the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

Lingoo Play requests microphone access only when you use a voice or speech-recognition feature. The app uses Android speech-recognition services to process the speech input needed for that feature. Lingoo Play does not intentionally store recordings or use the microphone in the background. Android and the selected speech-recognition provider may process audio and related information under their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Lingoo Play uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to Google’s policies and your device or regional choices. Learn more in Google’s partner-sites policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Network access

The app uses network access to load advertisements and to support speech-recognition services when you choose to use voice features. Lingoo Play does not operate its own account server or advertising database.

Children’s privacy

Lingoo Play is intended for a general audience and is not intended to require children to create accounts or submit personal information to the developer. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of apps and review the privacy choices available on their devices. Google AdMob and other third-party services may apply their own age-based safeguards and policies.

Data deletion

You can delete locally stored Lingoo Play data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Information processed by Google AdMob or Android speech-recognition services is subject to those providers’ controls and policies.

Security

We use the Android platform and reputable third-party services to operate the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so users should keep their device and operating system updated.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
